# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Glasstunnel contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to make
participation safe, respectful, and useful. Welcome questions, assume good intent,
give actionable feedback, respect privacy, and disagree with ideas without attacking
people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, publishing private information, sustained
disruption, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, community
spaces, and public interactions where someone represents the Glasstunnel project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **conduct@glasstunnel.io**. Include relevant
links or context, but do not collect or publish additional private information.
Maintainers will review reports in good faith, protect confidentiality as practical,
and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or ban an
individual depending on severity and pattern.

Maintainers must recuse themselves when they are directly involved in a report. No
one may retaliate against a person for raising a concern in good faith.
